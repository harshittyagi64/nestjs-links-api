# SkillSwap Requirements Quick Pass

## 5 Explicit Requirements

1. Users can browse providers by category.
2. Users can view provider profiles with ratings.
3. Users can book available time slots.
4. Users can pay through the platform.
5. Providers can set their availability and pricing.

## Ambiguities

1. Cancellation policy details are unclear:
   - Do providers define their own cancellation rules, or does the platform enforce a global policy?

2. Payment flow is unclear:
   - Is payment captured immediately during booking, or only after provider confirmation?

## Question for PM

How should double-booking prevention work when two users attempt to reserve the same provider time slot simultaneously?
